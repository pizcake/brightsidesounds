    About the music

    Made to be listened to slowly

    Brightside Sounds brings together 100 short pieces created as part of a research project into AI-composed music. Every piece shares a few qualities: an unhurried tempo, warm and open harmonies, and no sudden jumps in volume.

    There's no right way to listen. Start anywhere, let a whole collection play through, or come back to the one piece you like best.

    • Find a comfortable spot

      A chair by the window, a bench outside, or wherever you are right now.

    • Keep it soft

      A low volume suits these pieces. Headphones are nice but not necessary.

    • Let it play on

      Each collection flows from one piece into the next, so you can press play and leave it.
